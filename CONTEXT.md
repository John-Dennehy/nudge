# Nudge

An executive function scaffold designed for people with AuDHD to overcome task paralysis, systematically deconstruct overwhelming ideas, and convert failure into iterative learning.

## Language

**Task**:
A distinct unit of intention tracked through an explicit state machine from ideation to completion or learning.
_Avoid_: Todo, ticket, item, card

**Failed**:
The state reached when an active or paused task does not meet its time-bound target or stated outcome, serving as an explicit trigger for learning and adaptation rather than a punitive endpoint.
_Avoid_: Dropped, cancelled, abandoned, shelved

**Reviewed**:
The terminal state where a user reflects on an outcome (whether Succeeded or Failed) to extract lessons and adapt future strategy.
_Avoid_: Archived, closed, done, finished

**Unstick Mode**:
A minimal-friction interaction flow that bypasses planning overhead to generate a single, immediate two-minute micro-action from an unstructured brain-dump.
_Avoid_: Fast mode, quick add, panic button

**One Fork Rule**:
The design principle that overcomes executive paralysis by reducing activation energy to a single sub-atomic physical movement (e.g. putting one fork in the drawer) rather than tackling the whole task or "eating the frog".
_Avoid_: Eat the frog, high-impact first, MVP

**Plan Mode**:
The structured dialog flow that progressively evaluates and refines an idea against the SMART criteria framework.
_Avoid_: Task wizard, creation modal, detailed form

**Prerequisite**:
A blocking task that must reach completion before a dependent task can transition to Active.
_Avoid_: Dependency, blocker, requirement
