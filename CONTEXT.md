# Nudge

An executive function and decision-support second brain designed for people with AuDHD to overcome task paralysis, transform raw brain dumps into actionable SMART goals, and surface curated next steps based on current energy and emotional state.

## Language

**Brain Dump**:
Unstructured, low-friction text or voice input from the user that Nudge automatically parses and deconstructs into candidate goals.
_Avoid_: Task creation form, ticket submission

**SMART Goal**:
An actionable, atomic unit of work. A goal is not considered SMART if it can be reasonably split further (down to sub-atomic ~2-15 minute steps). Nudge is responsible for all splitting and parent-to-child tracking internally to protect the user from tree overwhelm.
_Avoid_: Epic, project, composite task, backlog item

**Clarification Card**:
A single, bite-sized multiple-choice question (with optional write-in) generated by Nudge to clarify an ambiguous idea or calibrate constraints without overwhelming the user.
_Avoid_: Form wizard, setup questionnaire, planning modal

**Confidence Score**:
A composite evaluation computed by Nudge measuring whether a candidate goal is sufficiently atomic, specific, achievable, and clear to be actionable.
_Avoid_: Completion percentage, progress bar

**Agreed Task**:
The single committed task currently being worked on by the user. Presented clearly without timers, countdown pressure, or judgment.
_Avoid_: In progress list, active sprint, WIP column

**Adaptive Nudges**:
A small, curated set of context-aware options surfaced to the user to choose their next step. Rather than a rigid, hardcoded menu, Nudge adapts and learns options based on individual needs and urgency (e.g., building momentum, clearing dread, high-leverage ROI, or emergency deconstruction of urgent deadlines).
_Avoid_: Fixed 3-task limit, rigid categories, Kanban columns, backlog ranking

**Failed**:
The honest state reached when a task is not completed as planned. Free of timers and judgment, it prompts constructive reflection ("Was it a reasonable goal?", "Was I overwhelmed?") and generates a calibrated replacement goal with a realistic deadline.
_Avoid_: Shelved, dropped, abandoned

**Reviewed**:
The reflective learning loop completed after any outcome. For successful tasks, it checks whether we are stretching outside our comfort zone or underestimating capacity; for failed tasks, it calibrates overwhelm and goal reasonableness.
_Avoid_: Archived, closed, done

**Quiet Progress**:
Gentle, visible indicators of non-obvious momentum (e.g. thoughts untangled, micro-steps completed, reflections recorded) designed to combat AuDHD time-blindness and the demoralizing feeling of having "done nothing today."
_Avoid_: Streaks, productivity score, leaderboard, gamification badges

## Communication Rule: Pure Plain English

All user-facing language must be entirely free of technical and productivity jargon. 
* Never show terms like "SMART criteria", "confidence score", "dependency graph", "state machine", or "triad selection" in the UI.
* Use warm, direct, conversational language:
  * Instead of *"Specific criterion unmet"*, say *"What does the first physical step look like?"*
  * Instead of *"Confidence score 45%"*, say *"This still feels a bit big or fuzzy."*
  * Instead of *"Deconstructing parent entity"*, say *"Let's make this easier to start."*
  * Instead of *"Task transitioned to Reviewed"*, say *"Saved your reflection."*
