# 3. One question at a time, as a hand of cards

Status: accepted

## Context

Forms, dropdowns and wizards cause decision fatigue. Nudge still needs answers to make goals startable.

## Decision

- Each Clarification Card asks about one missing part of one goal and offers three or four answers, plus a write-in that's read for the part being asked (minutes, a day, or words).
- Big goals get a "small first step" card instead. Picking one creates a step under the goal; when it finishes, the goal asks for the next step or "That's all of it".
- Cards are shown as a hand of tilted cards, inspired by the game Balatro: a tap or click picks one. Arrow keys (or h and l) move between them and Enter, Space or a number key picks. On a phone the hand scrolls sideways.
- Keys only work while the hand has focus, so they never get in the way of typing or a screen reader. "Not now" moves on to another goal's question.

## Consequences

- Most small goals need one or two questions, because the brain dump already answers the rest.
- The card hand is a real group of buttons with visible focus, rather than a custom widget.
