# Balatro-Style Multi-Modal Clarification Deck

When neurodivergent users are asked to clarify or plan tasks, conventional forms, dropdowns, and text wizards trigger cognitive fatigue and decision paralysis.

In Nudge, clarification questions are presented one at a time as a tactile, Balatro-inspired "hand" of option cards. Users can seamlessly navigate and select cards across multiple modalities:
- Keyboard: Arrow keys / numbers / Tab to cycle, Enter or Space to play/select.
- Mouse: Hover to elevate/inspect, click to select.
- Touch / Mobile: Swipe to browse, tap to select.
- Write-In: A clean fallback input immediately below the cards for custom specifications.

This transforms what would otherwise be a tedious administrative questionnaire into a satisfying, low-friction, playful interaction.
