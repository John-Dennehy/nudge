# Automated SMART Formulation and Progressive Clarification Cards

Traditional productivity tools force users to manually structure tasks into forms, fields, and sub-tasks. For users with AuDHD, this administrative barrier causes immediate executive shutdown and abandonment.

In Nudge, the user provides only raw, unstructured brain dumps. Nudge takes on the cognitive burden of parsing, structuring, and evaluating ideas against the SMART framework. When key constraints or definitions are missing, Nudge does not present a form or wizard; instead, it presents a single bite-sized Clarification Card with multiple-choice options (and write-in fallback) to resolve one ambiguity at a time until the goal achieves an actionable Confidence Score.
