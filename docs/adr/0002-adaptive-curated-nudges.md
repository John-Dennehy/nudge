# Adaptive Curated Nudges (Beyond Rigid Limits)

Traditional productivity systems either demand rigid prioritization queues or impose dogmatic formulas (e.g. always "eat the frog" or strictly pick 3 tasks). For neurodiverse and AuDHD users, cognitive capacity, emotional activation, and real-world urgency fluctuate constantly.

In Nudge, we reject arbitrary, prescriptive task limits. Instead, Nudge surfaces a small, intelligent selection of curated options that adapt and learn based on the individual's needs:
- **Momentum Builders ("One Fork")**: Sub-atomic actions with minimal activation energy to break inertia.
- **Dread Clearers ("Eat the Frog")**: Lingering or anxiety-inducing tasks presented when bandwidth allows.
- **High Leverage ("Bang for Buck")**: Highest ratio of impact to required effort.
- **Urgent Deconstruction**: When high-urgency, high-impact tasks cannot wait, Nudge surfaces them alongside an instant, bite-sized breakdown so the user isn't paralyzed by sheer magnitude.

Over time, Nudge learns which options best support the individual's flow state, presenting options as supportive leverage rather than a rigid cage.
