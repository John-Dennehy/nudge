# Continuous Reflection Across Both Failure and Success

In productivity tools designed for neurodivergent individuals, failure is commonly euphemized (e.g. "shelved", "dropped", "archived") to avoid triggering rejection sensitivity. In Nudge, we deliberately retain and elevate `Failed` as an explicit, first-class state in the task lifecycle, completely free of countdown timers, pressure, or moral judgment. 

However, reflection is not reserved solely for failure. Skill mastery and sustainable growth require evaluating all outcomes:
- **On Failure**: We honestly examine the breakdown without shame:
  1. *Was it a reasonable goal?*
  2. *Was I overwhelmed?*
  From this, Nudge generates a calibrated replacement goal with a realistic deadline.
- **On Success**: We evaluate whether we are growing or stagnating:
  1. *Did this task push me outside my comfort zone, or was it trivially safe?*
  2. *Did the estimation match reality?*

This transforms every completed or failed task into honest calibration data for future goal-sizing and confidence scoring.
