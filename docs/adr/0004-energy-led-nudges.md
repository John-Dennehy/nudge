# 4. A few nudges, chosen by energy

Status: accepted

## Context

Rigid rules ("always eat the frog", "pick three tasks") ignore how much capacity someone has today. A full ranked list causes overwhelm.

## Decision

- The user says how much energy they have right now: low, okay or good. It's kept on the device for a few hours.
- Nudge offers up to two (low), three (okay) or four (good) ready goals, each of a different kind: **due soon**, **quick win**, **lift a weight** (put back before, or a second try) and **moves something bigger** (a step of a bigger goal).
- Low energy only offers quick wins and anything due soon. If calibration shows starting is the hard part, quick wins come first.
- Every nudge says why it's there.

## Consequences

- The full list is still there, folded away under "See everything".
- "Lift a weight" uses how often something was put back and whether it's a second try, rather than just its age.
